## 6. Best Practices

1. **Personalize every message** — mention their company or recent work
2. **Keep it under 200 characters** for connection notes
3. **Never pitch in the connection request** — just the intro
4. **Wait for acceptance** before sending the full pitch
5. **Space out follows-ups** — 5 days minimum between messages
6. **Track responses** in a spreadsheet or CRM
7. **10-15 connection requests per day max** (LinkedIn rate limit)
